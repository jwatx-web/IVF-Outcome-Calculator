How It Works

Rooted in Clinical Reality

Our model uses established clinical benchmarks from thousands of IVF cycles, analyzing attrition rates at every stage — from mature eggs to blastocysts — to give you a realistic view of how a typical cycle progresses.

Personalized to Your Story

Your inputs matter. We use maternal age to estimate euploid rates and incorporate optional details like embryo grading to move from general averages to a projection that reflects your specific data. [1][2]

From Data to a Complete Picture

We model implantation rates — including the 5–10% boost from frozen transfers [6] — and factor in live birth rates after positive pregnancy [7] to give you a full-journey outlook.
